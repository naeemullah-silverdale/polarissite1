Safeguarding & Wellbeing

At Polaris Wellbeing Visits Ltd, we believe that the safety and wellbeing of every individual we support is paramount.

Safer Recruitment

All practitioners must undergo Enhanced DBS (Disclosure and Barring Service) checks before supporting clients. Our approach to safeguarding is proactive, maintaining a strong safeguarding-aware culture where the welfare of individuals remains at the heart of our practice.
