Our Support

Polaris Wellbeing Visits Ltd provides structured companionship and reassurance visits tailored to individual needs.

Companionship Visits

Friendly, meaningful visits that reduce loneliness, promote engagement and support emotional wellbeing.

Wellbeing Check-ins

Short visits to ensure safety, routine, hydration, nutrition reminders and peace of mind.

Community Access

Support accessing local social activities, walks and events – helping individuals remain connected.

Shopping Support

Support with weekly shopping, errands and other everyday tasks to maintain independence.
