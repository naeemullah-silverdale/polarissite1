What We Do

Polaris Wellbeing Visits Ltd provides friendly, reliable companionship and reassurance visits designed to help older adults remain confident, independent and socially connected.

Support may include:

  • Companionship visits
  • Short reassurance check-in visits
  • Appointment escort support
  • Shopping support and errands
  • Reassurance updates for families

Who Our Service Supports

  • Older adults living independently
  • Adults with Learning Disabilities
  • Adults with Mental-Health Needs
  • Families living at a distance
