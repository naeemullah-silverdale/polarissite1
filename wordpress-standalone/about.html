About Us

Our Identity & Heritage

Polaris has guided travellers for centuries, offering direction and reassurance in uncertain times.

We chose the name Polaris because that reflects what we aim to be for the people we support which is a steady, reliable presence helping individuals navigate daily life with confidence.

Polaris Wellbeing Visits Ltd was founded by experienced care professionals with backgrounds in adult care, safeguarding and frontline support.

Our Approach

  • Safeguarding-led
  • Professional and reliable
  • Person-centred
